2)  Assumptions

```{=html}
<!-- -->
```
1.  *Linearity* *Assumption ... Y일 확률 p와 X의 선형 관계\
    *$\text{g}{\text{\{p}\left( \text{x}\text{1}\text{,…,x}\text{p} \right)}\text{\}~=~}\text{β}_{0} + \text{β}_{1}\text{x}\text{1}$ +...+${\ \text{β}}_{p}\text{x}\text{p}$

2.  *Independence* *Assumption* ... Y~1~,.., Y~n~ are independent random variables

3.  *Logit link function* ... p와 X는 logit으로 연결\
    ${\text{g}\left( \text{p} \right)\text{~=~ln}}\left( \frac{\text{p}}{\text{1}\text{-}\text{p}} \right)$
