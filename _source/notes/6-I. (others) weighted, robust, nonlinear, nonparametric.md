5)  Model estimation (model structure를 모를 때) \~ nonparametric regression

-   Data: {(x~11~,.., x~1p~),Y~1~},....,{(x~n1~,.., x~np~ ),Y~n~}

-   Model: Y~i~ = f(x~i1~,...,x~ip~) + e~i~, i =1,...,n

-   **f(x~i1~,...,x~ip~) [unknown]{.underline}**

-   ***Curse of dimensionality** : 같은 accuary를 얻으려면 데이터가 많아질수록(차원이 높아질수록) 지수적으로 sample size 증가 (p = 1 & n = 300 == p=5 & n=30000)*

6)  Estimation of non-parametric regression

-   **Generalized Additive Model (GAM)**

-   **Model:** Y~i~ = f(x~i1~,...,x~ip~) + e~i~, i =1,...,n with f(x~1~,...,x~p~) = ${\alpha + \ f}_{1}$(x~1~) + ...+ $f_{p}$(x~p~) where $f_{1}$**, ..,** $f_{p}$ are unknown but **smooth functions**

-   **Model Estimation (**예시 Backfitting algorithm)

1.  Initialize: $\widehat{\alpha}$, ${\widehat{f}}_{1}$,..., ${\widehat{f}}_{p}$

2.  Iterate until convergence: For j=1,..,p

3.  ${\check{R}}_{i} =$ Y~i~ - $\widehat{\alpha}$ - $\sum_{k \neq j}^{}{{\widehat{f}}_{k}(x_{\text{ki}})}$ and estimate ${\widehat{f}}_{j}$ from regressing ${\check{R}}_{i}\ \sim$ $x_{\text{ji}}$

-   ***gam() in mgcv or gam library***
